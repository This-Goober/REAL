Most AI video tools generate a video. REAL edits yours, and shows its work.
Four steps, four files you can read. A notebook: your script, with the shots marked inline.
A catalogue: every clip described, anything unverified marked unknown.
Then the reel. Every picture is pinned to a word, not a timestamp, so re-recording the voice moves nothing you approved.
Last, a real timeline for Final Cut Pro or DaVinci Resolve: editable clips, titles, lanes. What it cannot represent, it refuses.
