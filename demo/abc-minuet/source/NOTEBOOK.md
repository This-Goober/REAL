## hook

The creator's own call: open on the music. The four bars play on piano with no
voice, over the engraved score, so the viewer hears the tune before any letters
appear.

## the header

<The lines at the top set the rules. M is the meter, three beats in every bar. L is the default length, so a plain letter lasts one beat. And K is the key, C, which means no sharps or flats.>
<show image of the same ABC text with only the header lines in red and a short gray label beside each: "meter: 3 beats a bar", "a letter = 1 beat", "key: C" #abc-header>

## pitch

<Each letter is a note. A capital C is middle C, and a lowercase c is the C one octave higher.>
<show image of a treble staff with two notes, middle C on its ledger line and the C an octave up in the third space, the letters "C" and "c" in large monospace type under each #pitch-c>

## length

<A slash cuts a note in half. So C slash is an eighth note, half a beat, and two of them fill the space of one plain C.>
<show image of two panels side by side: "C" over a single quarter note, and "C/ C/" over two beamed eighth notes, the same width on the page, with "1 beat" under both #length-slash>

## the bars

Slow and even: one bar at a time, the bar lit up red in the score and in the
text at the same moment, everything else gray.

<Bar one is G for one beat, then C, D, E, F as four quick eighth notes. One beat, plus four halves, makes three.>
<show image of the score with bar 1 in red and the rest gray, and under it the ABC line with "G C/ D/ E/ F/" in red #bar-1>
<Bar two is G, C, C. Three plain letters, three beats.>
<show image of the same score and ABC line with bar 2 in red, same framing #bar-2>
<Bar three is the same shape as bar one, a step higher: A, then F, G, A, B.>
<show image of the same score and ABC line with bar 3 in red, same framing #bar-3>
<And bar four jumps up to the high c, then back down to C, C.>
<show image of the same score and ABC line with bar 4 in red, same framing #bar-4>

## the check

<Every bar should add up to three. If one doesn't, then a note is missing, or a bar line is in the wrong place.>
<show image of the four bars written as text with a small "3" under each bar, all four in gray, checkmarks in red #bar-sums>

## payoff

Let the music run clean at the end, no voice over it.
