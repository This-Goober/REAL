That's the opening of the Minuet in G from Anna Magdalena Bach's notebook Here it is in C written as a single line of text The lines at the top set the rules M is the meter three beats in every bar L is the default length so a plain letter lasts one beat And K is the key C which means no sharps or flats Each letter is a note A capital C is middle C and a lowercase c is the C one octave higher A slash cuts a note in half So C slash is an eighth note half a beat and two of them fill the space of one plain C Bar one is G for one beat then C D E F as four quick eighth notes One beat plus four halves makes three Bar two is G C C Three plain letters three beats Bar three is the same shape as bar one a step higher: A then F G A B And bar four jumps up to the high c then back down to C C Every bar should add up to three If one doesn't then a note is missing or a bar line is in the wrong place That's the whole idea Four bars of music in about forty characters Here it is again
