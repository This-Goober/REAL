Two fixes: revise.py now REFUSES `media L0 = <an audio id>` with a message naming the right instruction instead of silently replacing a picture with a sound file; and there is now an `add audio track = <id> at <sceneId>` verb that positions the track at that scene's start and sizes it to the take, warning if the take is shorter than the picture it has to cover.

  7. LAYERS, NOT CUTS — corrected 2026-08-27 on the director's note. The first version of this segment cut G1->G2->G3->G4: Tunable, then the Bach take, then the ear image as a full base shot, then BACK to the Bach take. That reads as switching away and returning.   8. AUDIO IS PER-SEGMENT, NOT PER-SHOT — also corrected on the director's note. The storyboard used to put an AUDIO dropdown on every scene block, which implied the narration is chosen shot by shot. It is not: it is one continuous read on connected lane -1 spanning the whole segment. The HTML now chooses it once, in an AUDIO panel at the top; a scene only shows an audio control when it genuinely has its own bed under the voice.
  9. ⚠ ONE SHOT PER SCENE, BECAUSE to_story.py ONLY READS THE FIRST LANE-0 LAYER. Found while preparing this segment: to_story emits exactly one beat per scene and takes `base = next(...)` — the first lane-0 layer. Any further lane-0 layers are neither beats nor overlays, so they vanish with no warning. The full draft.json has 23 lane-0 layers inside M5; run as-is it would produce ONE shot and silently drop 22. That is the single most important thing this smoke test surfaced, and it needs fixing in Module 2 (split lane-0 layers into scenes at hand-off) or in to_story.py (emit a beat per lane-0 layer) before the full video goes through.
  10. VOICE — SOLVED, and it did not need a schema change. Module 3's story.json has a top-level `connected` array for anything spanning beats (narration, music, full-width captions), with `src`, `lane` (negative for audio), `offset` from the START OF THE TIMELINE, optional `start` as an in-point into the source, `duration`, and `role`. to_story.py already copies draft.connected straight through. So the voice belongs in draft.json as `connected` and reaches Final Cut as a real connected clip on lane -1. The earlier 'picture only' note was wrong: the capability existed, Module 2 just never wrote it. Both takes are now declared, with take 1 in-pointed at 10.310s so no trimming is needed on disk.
  11. BLACK SCREEN — G5 is `kind: blank`, which to_story turns into a named gap. That is the correct shape: picture blank, narration continuing over it. Six seconds is long; the catalogue would rather have one card there.
  12. video-557_excerpt.MOV must be copied into Videos/ on the Mac. The in-points (0.0 and 4.9) are offsets into the 10s EXCERPT, not the 370MB original.
  13. TWO to_story.py QUIRKS worth knowing: (a) `if base.get('in')` is falsy for an in-point of exactly 0.0, so a deliberate 0.0 is indistinguishable from unset — harmless, since 0 is the default; (b) a scene's `notes` becomes the gap's clip NAME in Final Cut, so a long note turns into an unreadable clip label. Keep notes short on blank scenes and put the reasoning here.
