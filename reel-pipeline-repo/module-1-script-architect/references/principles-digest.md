# Principles digest — OFFLINE FALLBACK ONLY

The live list lives in the project's editing-principles guide, if it keeps one.
Read that first. Use this digest only when the project is unreachable, and
tell the director you are working from a snapshot that may be stale.

Snapshot date: 2026-08-02.

Principles are scoped **[global]**, **[genre]** (this series: education-style,
mixed demonstration + explanation), or **[one-off]** (this-video feedback, never
generalized). Never promote a one-off yourself.

The ones that bear on planning a script:

1. **Black-space duration budget** [genre] — a blank never longer than one content
   segment (a phrase-level beat).
2. **Conceptual hook** [genre, advisory] — openings must create a "why?", not
   "tell." Suggest reorders; the director decides.
3. **Mid-word cuts** [global] — b-roll switches never on phrase boundaries or
   breaths; always ~40% into the anchor word.
4. **Demo audio ducking** [global] — demo audio is a quiet bed (~30%) under
   narration, full when narration stops. Never hard-muted.
5. **"Moment" cues** [global, human-in-loop] — peak-moment effects are the
   director's intuition. Propose ranked candidates; never pick.
7. **Simultaneity snapping** [global] — cues within ~0.3s snap to one frame unless
   marked as deliberate stagger.
8. **Blank-frequency budget** [genre, halt-and-ask] — 1–2 blanks per video max;
   over budget, halt and offer the menu: (a) add imagery, (b) extend the
   neighbouring screen, (c) extend the b-roll underlay.
9. **Screen-filled default / conceptual-blank exception** [genre] — blanks need a
   deliberate reason. A sub-second breath of black (~0.3s) before a reveal is good
   rhythm and does not count against the budget.
10. **Time-expensive script phrases** [genre, script-review flag] — a subordinate
    phrase that eats seconds and adds only flavour forces a stimulus need. Flag it
    with two options: keep it and add a stimulus, or reword. Fill imagery must be
    relevant to the words; a mismatched leftover image is worse than the blank.
11. **Audio is editable too** [global] — pauses can be compressed. Cutting silence
    is often better than filling it.
12. **Demo the options** [global] — when the director must choose, show the
    options rather than describing them.
13. **Pipeline order** [global] — superseded in practice by the two-clock model
    this pipeline settled on: time is defined before visuals, by estimate at
    planning and by measurement once recorded; word anchors are the invariant.

Division of labor: the director owns script, voice, all media selection,
overall feel, and final timing taste. The tooling owns alignment, timing,
sequencing, and execution. Uncovered situation → flag and ask, never guess.
