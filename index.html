想

The idea

構想

Most AI video tools generate a video. REAL is a crew for a human director: it plans, catalogues, storyboards and compiles the director's own script and footage into a real, editable timeline for Final Cut Pro or DaVinci Resolve.

The human makes every creative call. The agent does the bookkeeping, and when it cannot be sure, it refuses to guess. Every decision has one owner, every handoff is one file you can read, and every step validates rather than trusts.

“A random error looks like noise. A coherent error reads as an editorial fact.”The finding behind every design decision
四

Four steps, four files

起承転結

Each step owns one kind of decision and hands the next step one file. Nothing downstream re-decides what was settled upstream.

起
1 · /real-brainstorm

NOTEBOOK.md

Your script, with the shots marked inline. What comes after what, and what sits on top of what. No times, no files.

承
2 · /real-create-catalogue

asset-catalog.json

Every clip in your folder described: what it shows, where it earns its place. Anything unverified is marked unknown, never guessed.

転
3 · /real-storyboarding

reel.json + the tracks page

Every picture is pinned to a word, not a timestamp, so re-recording the voice moves nothing you approved. One owner per decision; you correct it by clicking.

結
4 · /real-compile

.fcpxml and .otio

A real timeline: editable clips, titles, lanes. What it cannot represent, it refuses. It never renders video; the editor does the rest.

The notebook grammar is the only syntax anyone learns. One angle bracket is one thing on screen or in the ear; a bar means at the same time.

言

Word anchors

言葉の錨

The word is the identity. The timestamp is derived.

A caption, a still or a cut is placed on a spoken word, not on a second. The storyboard runs first on an estimated clock; once the narration is recorded, the recording is measured word by word and the clock is swapped. The words move, the placements follow, and nothing is re-planned. Re-timing never re-decides.

二

One storyboard, two editors

二つの編集

The Paganini No. 23 tutorial, cut from a violinist's own recording, compiled once to FCPXML and once to OpenTimelineIO, then rendered by Final Cut Pro and by DaVinci Resolve. The two renders were measured against each other, frame by frame.

Demo folder and measurements: the prompts, the source media, both timelines, the measurement report and the scripts, so the result can be rebuilt and re-measured.

Try it

試す
  • Open the live interactive storyboardA real review page from a field session: embedded thumbnails, per-scene layers, candidate alternatives, click to correct.
  • The user manualWhat to bring to each step, what happens, and what you get out.
  • The capability journalOne capability, one measurement, one entry at a time.
  • The repositorySkills, benchmark, demos, paper sources. MIT license.

The four skills run in Claude. Clone, package, upload, then start a session in the folder that holds your footage.

git clone https://github.com/This-Goober/REAL.git
cd REAL/skills/batch-2/skills
for s in real-*; do zip -r "$s.skill" "$s"; done
# upload each .skill: Claude → Settings → Capabilities → Skills
  1. Put everything in one folder on the Mac that runs your editor.
  2. /real-brainstorm your idea, then go shoot the shotlist. Record the narration early.
  3. /real-create-catalogue the folder and approve the rename list.
  4. /real-storyboarding, then review by clicking in the tracks page.
  5. /real-compile, import the timeline, finish the edit in your editor.
